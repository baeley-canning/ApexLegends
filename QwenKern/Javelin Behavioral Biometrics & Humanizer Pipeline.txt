1. REACTION TIME DISTRIBUTION
================================================================================

Human Baseline:
- Distribution: Normal distribution N(250ms, σ=75ms)
- Range: 180ms - 400ms typical
- Min reaction time: ~150ms (experienced players)
- Max reaction time: ~450ms (casual/fatigued players)

Javelin Analysis:
- Reaction time distribution is a key ML feature
- Deviations from N(250ms, σ=75ms) trigger behavioral flags
- Context-aware analysis (combat vs. idle states)
- Kill-streak modifiers applied

Implementation Targets:
- Mean: 250ms (±10ms variance per session)
- Std: 75ms (maintain within 65-85ms range)
- Min: 180ms (avoid going below 150ms)
- Max: 400ms (avoid exceeding 450ms)

================================================================================
2. MICRO-JITTER PATTERNS
================================================================================

Mouse Micro-Jitter:
- Distribution: N(0, σ=1.5°) at 60Hz
- Frequency: 60Hz sampling rate
- Amplitude: ~1.5° standard deviation
- Tremor frequency: ~8Hz (natural hand tremor)

Human Baseline Patterns:
- Micro-jitter is natural and unavoidable in human input
- Javelin expects this variance - too-perfect input is suspicious
- Jitter should increase with fatigue (kill-streak decay)
- Different for different game states (aiming vs. idle)

Implementation Targets:
- σ = 1.5° (degrees of rotation)
- Frequency = 60Hz (match display refresh)
- Add tremor component at ~8Hz
- Increase jitter during extended sessions

3. MOUSE VELOCITY PROFILES
================================================================================

Velocity Distribution:

- Smooth acceleration/deceleration patterns
- Natural "start/stop" smoothness
- Velocity variance scales with movement distance

Human Patterns:
- Acceleration: Smooth S-curve for short movements
- Deceleration: Exponential decay near target
- Velocity peaks at ~70-80% of movement distance
- Natural variance in peak velocity (±15%)

================================================================================
4. KILL-STREAK DECAY
================================================================================

Decay Pattern:
- Exponential accuracy reduction over kill streaks
- Peak performance: first 3-5 kills
- Gradual decline: kills 6-15
- Fatigue effect: kills 15+ show measurable degradation

Decay Function:
- Accuracy = base_accuracy * e^(-streak/decay_constant)
- Base accuracy: ~85-90% for good players
- Decay constant: ~8-12 kills (typical)
- Fatigue penalty: additional 5-10% reduction after 20+ kills

================================================================================
5. MISS INJECTION
================================================================================

Miss Rate Calculation:
- Base probability: ~15% (varies by rank)
- Distance-weighted: farther targets = higher miss rate
- Rank adjustment: lower ranks = more misses (matches human baseline)
- Streak decay: missed shots increase with streak length

Distance-Weighted Formula:
miss_rate = base_rate * (1 + distance_factor * target_distance)

Rank-Based Adjustment:
- Bronze: +20% miss rate
- Silver: +10% miss rate
- Gold: baseline (0%)
- Platinum: -5% miss rate
- Diamond: -10% miss rate
- Apex: -15% miss rate
