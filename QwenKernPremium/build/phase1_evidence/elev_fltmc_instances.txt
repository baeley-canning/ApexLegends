    instances -- Lists all instances or the instances associated with a
                 Filter or Volume

    C:\WINDOWS\system32\fltMC.exe instances [-f filterName ]|[-v volumeName ]

        The -f flag denotes that the instances associated with the filter
        identified by filterName should be listed

        The -v flag denotes that the instances associated with the volume
        identified by volumeName should be listed

        The full information on each instance will be listed
